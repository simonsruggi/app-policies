# Fastara - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 13, 2026**

## Overview

Fastara ("the App") is developed by Simone Ruggiero. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

## Data Collection

**Fastara does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** All your fasting history, weight entries and preferences are stored entirely on your device.

### What the App stores locally on your device:
- **Fasting history**: start/end times of your fasts and the protocol used.
- **Weight entries**: the weights you choose to log, used only for your personal chart.
- **Preferences**: your selected protocol, reminders and profile answers from onboarding.

### What the App does NOT do:
- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not upload your fasting or weight data to any server

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses **RevenueCat** for managing in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Health Disclaimer

Fastara provides educational information about intermittent fasting. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult your doctor before changing your diet.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
