## Navigation and Weather Disclaimer

Scirocco is a navigation aid, **not** a certified nautical chart system. It does not replace official charts, instruments and publications required on board, nor the judgement of the person in command of the vessel.

Weather and sea forecasts come from numerical models and may be inaccurate or out of date. Before going out, always check the official marine bulletins and the local harbour authority ordinances.

GPS positions, tracks, distances and fuel estimates depend on your device and may contain errors.
